Spot the Imposter Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: Spot the Imposter doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you type stays on your phone, or goes only to the other phones in your game.

Who we are

Spot the Imposter is a party game for Android made by an independent developer. If you have any questions about this policy, email neil@neiljsmith.co.uk.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your phone so they're there next time:

  • the players' names and the order of play,
  • your game settings and chosen decks,
  • your own words, if you add any,
  • scores, and which words have come up recently (so they don't repeat too soon).

We never receive any of this. Deleting the app deletes it. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include it in your phone's own backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google, and we can't see it.

Playing on several phones

When you host or join a game, the phones talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot. The names of the people playing, the cards, votes and scores are sent between the phones in that game, and only while it's being played. Nothing goes through our servers. We don't have any.

People can also join from a web browser by scanning the host's QR code. The page comes from the host's phone over the same local network, and it stores only a random ID and the player's name in that browser, so they can rejoin if the connection drops.

The host's phone announces the game on the local network (showing the room code and the host's first player name) so that other phones nearby can find it.

Your own words

If you use "Share / back up" on your own words, the app opens Android's share sheet and you choose where the list goes, for example email or a cloud drive. We don't receive it.

Purchases

Unlocks are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app what you've bought. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of your information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect the phones in a game over your local network, and for Google Play purchases.

The app doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, except a file you choose yourself when importing your own words.

Children

Spot the Imposter is a family party game, but it isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
